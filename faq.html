Getting Started

🎮

How do I start playing quizzes?

Super easy! Just click on "Explore" in the menu, pick a category that interests you, and hit "Start Quiz". No signup needed to try our demo quiz! 🎉

💡 Pro tip: Create an account to track your scores and compete on the leaderboard!

✍️

Do I need to create an account?

Nope! You can play our demo quizzes without an account. BUT... if you want to save your progress, see your stats, and climb the leaderboard, you'll want to sign up! It's free and takes just 30 seconds. ⚡

🆓

Is QuizVerse really free?

100% FREE! 🎊 This is a college project made with ❤️ by students who love learning. No hidden fees, no premium plans, no tricks. Just pure quiz fun!

🎯

Playing Quizzes

⏱️

How much time do I get per question?

Most questions give you 30 seconds to answer. Watch that timer! ⏰ When the time runs out, we automatically move to the next question.

Don't panic though - 30 seconds is plenty of time if you know your stuff! 😎

🔄

Can I retake a quiz?

Absolutely! Take quizzes as many times as you want. Practice makes perfect! 💪 Each attempt might have different questions, so you'll keep learning new things.

📱

Can I play on my phone?

Yes! QuizVerse works perfectly on phones, tablets, and computers. Play anywhere, anytime! 📱💻

🏆

Scoring & Leaderboards

💯

How is my score calculated?

Simple math! You get 1 point for each correct answer. Your final score is shown as both:

  • Raw score (e.g., 8 out of 10)
  • Percentage (e.g., 80%)

The faster you answer correctly, the better you'll rank on the leaderboard! 🚀

👑

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Just create an account and start scoring! Your best scores automatically appear on the leaderboard. Beat other players to climb to the top! 🌟

🎯 Pro Strategy: Speed matters! If two players have the same score, the faster one ranks higher.

For Admins

🔑

How do I access the Admin Panel?

If you're an admin, here's how to get in:

  1. Go to the Login page
  2. You'll see a User / Admin toggle switch below the password field
  3. Click the "Admin" side of the toggle to switch to admin mode
  4. Enter your admin email and password
  5. Hit Sign In — you'll be taken straight to the Admin Panel! 🎉

⚠️ Important: If you try to log in as an admin without switching the toggle, the system will show a friendly warning and automatically flip the toggle for you. Just hit Sign In again!

🔒 Note: Only accounts with admin privileges can access the Admin Panel. Regular user accounts won't be able to switch to admin mode.
